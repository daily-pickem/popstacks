## Full ASO Description (4000 chars max)

Step inside **PopStacks: Art Toy Atelier** — a calm, deeply satisfying hexagonal logic-sorting puzzle where every stack you merge casts collectible designer vinyl figurines for your personal workshop shelves!

Join **Ollie the Clay Sculptor** at the studio workbench. Drag stacks of soft-touch matte vinyl casting discs from your dispenser tray onto the hexagonal board. Place a stack next to matching top colors and watch them automatically arc and stack together in a rhythmic cascade of warm marimba chimes!

When a stack reaches **10 identical vinyl discs**, it peels and pops in a tactile ceramic burst—pouring molten vinyl into your figurine mold and unveiling hidden disc layers underneath to trigger chain-reaction cascades across the board.
